# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

android

## Stack

Flutter (targeting Android SDK 26+, cross-platform ready for iOS)

## Users

Recreational and beginner/intermediate runners who listen to music while running and want an engaging, immersive connection between their strides and their sound, without tedious setup, calibration, or intrusive screens.

## Product Purpose

STRADIO transforms physical running steps into real-time interactive audio feedback and stem synchronization, turning physical movement into music. Success means runners experience instantaneous audio-tactile synchronicity (<30 ms step detection, <50 ms audio trigger latency) that makes continuous running enjoyable, motivating, and effortless.

## Positioning

Unlike conventional fitness apps (which focus on distance, pace, calories, and spreadsheets) or passive music streaming platforms, STRADIO is an experience-first, movement-powered audio companion. Its distinct mechanism: your physical cadence directly modulates audio stems and generates rhythmic step accents in real time.

## Key Workflows & Surfaces

1. **Pre-Run (Surface 1):** Zero-friction launch (<3 seconds from tap to audio). Select Sound Pack ("Midnight Pulse" vs. "Cartoon Squeak") on calm alabaster cards, then trigger the solid Midnight dock pill (`Swipe to start`).
2. **Active Run (Surface 2):** Dual-tone stacked header (`Stride / Rhythm`), central 280px circular acoustic dial synced to foot strikes, two borderless glanceable metrics (`Steps` and `Cadence`), and a recessed bottom dock with a safe 1.5-second hold-to-end control.
3. **Background Pocket Mode:** Audio engine and step detection continue seamlessly with the screen locked in a pocket or armband via an Android Foreground Service.
4. **Summary (Surface 3):** Dual-tone header (`Cadence / Flow`), wide-spaced 3px vertical cadence stems, and two summary cards (`Rhythm Sync` and `Cadence Focus`).

## Durable Constraints & Commitments

- **Zero-Friction Ignition:** No sign-up walls, tutorials, or mandatory profile setup before running.
- **Pocket Immunity:** 100% of the movement-to-audio experience works with the screen turned off.
- **Daylight Instrument Minimalism:** Warm Ivory (`#F5F0E8`) daylight canvas (60%+ open space), Recessed Stone (`#EAE4DC`) instrument wells, solid Midnight (`#191923`) bottom pill docks (15.37:1 contrast), and whisper-quiet sentence-case typography (`Sora` 500/600 + `Inter` 400/500).
- **Runner Ergonomics:** All touch targets at least 48 dp (56 dp for primary dock pills) for sweaty or moving hands; tabular numeric font alignment (`tnum`) to prevent counter jitter during cadence changes.
